How it works

From history to handoff

01 / Import

Review what you keep

Bring in supported ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or WhatsApp exports and review the context before saving it.

02 / Organize

Give context a project

Keep relevant memories alongside decisions, tasks, and handoff notes in the vault folder you choose.

03 / Connect

Choose what to share

Grant a compatible assistant access through MCP and use project scopes or read-only permissions to limit it.

Local use requires no EleSync account. Vault encryption is optional. Connected assistants may read the context you grant them.
