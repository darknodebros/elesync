EleSync keeps selected project notes, decisions, and conversations in a local vault on your computer.

Review what goes into the vault, organize it by project, and choose which approved AI assistants can access it.

=======

EleSync keeps selected context in a local vault so your projects, memories, and imported knowledge stay under your control.

You decide which AI assistants can access that vault and what they can see, instead of handing over all your context by default.
